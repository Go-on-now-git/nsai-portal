The Machines Never Sleep. You Still Have To.

September 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Yesterday New Jersey hit the massive DataOne AI data center with a $1.07 million fine — the largest ever levied against a data center in the state — for running 62 natural-gas generators without permits. Same week, the House voted 417-3 to make AI data centers pay their own power bills instead of passing them to your utility statement. Grassroots groups have now blocked or delayed some $68 billion in US data center projects this quarter alone.

The machines are running hot. Literally — gigawatts of it, 24 hours a day, in your backyard, on your grid.

And the humans tending those machines? Also running hot. The AI industry runs on a culture of permanent urgency: ship the model, scale the cluster, answer the page at 3am. Your brain was not designed for the notification economy. Nobody's nervous system evolved to process 400 Slack messages, a production incident, and the slow background dread of being automated — daily, for years.

AI doesn't need sleep. It doesn't get cortisol spikes. It never lies awake rehearsing a conversation from 2019. You do. And the most underrated performance upgrade in tech isn't a faster GPU — it's a nervous system that can actually power down.
