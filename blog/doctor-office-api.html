Your Doctor's Office Is Becoming an API

September 23, 2026 · 4 min read

Yesterday, the doctor's office got a little more optional. Rebody Health announced nationwide access to physician-reviewed NAD+ injections (September 22, 2026) — online intake, licensed clinician review, then the treatment ships to your home. No infusion clinic, no waiting room. It's the same intake-and-review pipeline the company already uses for testosterone and GLP-1 prescriptions. The clinic visit, unbundled into an API call.

This isn't one company — it's the whole industry's direction. Dexcom's Stelo is the first FDA-cleared over-the-counter continuous glucose monitor, and the G7 15-day system stretched clinical CGM wear past two weeks of continuous metabolic data. Oura's 2026 women's-health AI update added fertility-window estimation from a ring. Diagnostics left the lab years ago. Now treatment is leaving the building too.

The pattern is the same one this blog keeps finding: measure at home, consult online, treat by mail. At-home lab testing tells you what's actually wrong instead of what's convenient to guess. Telehealth puts a licensed clinician and a real prescription behind it without the commute. Your health stack is becoming software — versioned, data-driven, and delivered.
